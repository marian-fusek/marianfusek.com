What A‑Void stores

The app does not require an account. It stores the information you choose to enter into timelines, including names, groups, notes, dates, reminder plans, recurrence settings, custom types, custom groups and whether a timeline has been resolved.

It also stores app preferences such as appearance, notification settings, haptics, sorting and A‑Void Plus feature settings. These are kept on the device.

No ads or tracking. The current app contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

You control the content. A‑Void can contain personal or sensitive information if you enter it—for example information about documents, insurance, subscriptions, medication or appointments. Do not enter information you do not want stored in the locations described below.

When data leaves the device

Optional iCloud Sync

If you have A‑Void Plus and turn on iCloud Sync, timeline, custom type and custom group data are copied to A‑Void’s private iCloud container so your Apple devices can stay aligned. The app does not operate a separate A‑Void account server.

Automatic backups

If you turn on A‑Void Plus Automatic Backup, A‑Void writes rolling JSON copies to local app storage and, when available, iCloud Documents. The app keeps a rolling maximum of seven copies. These copies may contain the same timeline data, notes and dates as the app.

Manual exports

You can export a JSON backup through the iOS share sheet. Once you share or save that file, it is controlled by the destination you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive, email or another app.

Widgets and notifications

Widgets use a local shared app-group snapshot containing selected timeline names, groups, dates and countdown information. Local notifications are scheduled by iOS and may show a timeline name and reminder message on your devices or Lock Screen, depending on your settings.

Purchases

A‑Void Plus purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. A‑Void uses Apple’s StoreKit to read product and entitlement status. Payment card details are handled by Apple, not collected by A‑Void.

Website

The marketing website does not intentionally use advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. It loads the General Sans web font from Fontshare, so your browser may make a technical request to that provider. The website has no A‑Void account or support-ticket form.

Deletion and retention

A‑Void provides an Erase all data action that removes local timelines, groups and reusable types and cancels the app’s scheduled reminder notifications. If iCloud Sync is enabled, the deletion is prepared for synchronization to your other devices.

Manual exports are separate files and remain wherever you saved or shared them. Automatic backup files are separate copies too; they are replaced as the rolling backup set changes and are not the same thing as the live local timeline.

A‑Void does not operate a support ticketing system or separate support database. If you contact us by email, your message is processed through email services so we can respond. We delete support correspondence from our mailbox when it is no longer needed. Email providers may retain copies according to their own policies.

Your choices

You can use A‑Void without enabling iCloud Sync, Automatic Backup or widgets. You can deny notification permission, export your data, erase local data and stop using the app at any time.
