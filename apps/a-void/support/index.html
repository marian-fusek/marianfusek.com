Contact

Email email@marianfusek.com and include “A‑Void Support” in the subject if your mail app does not add it automatically.

There is no support account or ticket portal. Support is handled by email, directly by Marián Fůsek.

What to include

A short description of what you expected, what happened instead, your iPhone model, iOS version and A‑Void version will usually make troubleshooting faster.

Please do not send your Apple Account password, payment-card details, full passport or ID numbers, medical records, or other sensitive information that is not needed to explain the issue.

Purchases and subscriptions

A‑Void Plus purchases are processed by Apple. Apple controls billing, subscription cancellation and refunds. If the issue is a charge, renewal, refund or App Store transaction, use Apple’s subscription and purchase support as well as emailing us if you need help understanding the A‑Void feature.

For the features included in Plus, see the Terms and the A‑Void Plus description in the app.

Support and privacy

A‑Void does not operate a support ticketing system or separate support database. If you email us, the message is processed through email services so we can read and answer it. We delete support correspondence from our mailbox when it is no longer needed; email providers may retain copies under their own policies.

For the app’s local storage, iCloud, widget, notification, backup and export behavior, read the Privacy page.
