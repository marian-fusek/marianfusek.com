[NEW] Chirp™

Meet Chirp. Birdie’s little voice.

Let Birdie do the sensing and Chirp do the talking.
Your air readings stay visible, simple and one tap away.

What it does

Chirp shows Birdie Pro’s readings right in the room. CO₂, temperature, humidity and more.
Tap to move between readings. Each view stays simple: one icon, one number, one unit.

How it behaves

In the Birdie app, you choose what Chirp normally shows and how it reacts. Stay on your selected reading. Switch when something meaningful changes. Or show the change briefly, then return to your default.

  1. ① Open the Birdie app with Chirp nearby.
  2. ② Chirp found → Choose Birdie → Connect
  3. ③ From there Chirp belongs to that Birdie.

The app handles settings, preferred readings and display behavior.

The deeds

Made to stay on

Chirp uses a 2.13” black-and-white e-paper display. It only needs meaningful power when the screen changes, so your reading can stay visible all day without running a conventional display.

  • 250 × 122 px
  • 48.5 × 23.7 mm active area
  • Black / white
  • 0.3 sec partial refresh
  • Optional front light
  • 2.13” Good Display e-paper

Charge it. Forget about it.

A small rechargeable battery powers Chirp, with a target of 6–12 months between charges under normal use. Pop it off its magnetic wall mount, plug in USB-C and put it back when charged.

Birdie Pro itself already follows the same rechargeable, wireless approach and is rated for up to one year per charge.

Stick it anywhere*

Chirp doesn’t need to sit beside the bird.

A thin magnetic backplate can go up with 3M tape or a screw. Snap Chirp onto it, take it off when it needs charging, and place it wherever the reading is easiest to see.

Next to Birdie makes the speech-bubble idea come alive. Across the room makes the number useful.

*Easy, easy
