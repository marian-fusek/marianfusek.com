CORNDOGGY × DIGITAL

More ways to play with the doodles.

Simple ideas built around the thing people already love: Corndoggy’s drawings.

No social network. No complicated setup. Just more ways for fans to use, share and play with Corndoggy on game day.

01

Corndoggy Maker

A tiny design tool for making Corndoggy avatars, Stories, posts and wallpapers.

Medium
How it works

1. Pick a format: avatar, square post or Story.

2. Add Corndoggy stickers from the built-in gallery.

3. Move, resize, rotate and layer them. Add text, colors and a background.

4. Export or share the finished graphic straight to social apps.

Audience

Fans who share NFL reactions, memes, fantasy trash talk or game-day posts.

Players

Solo

Uses existing

The full Corndoggy sticker library.

Needs new

A few backgrounds, type styles and decorative pieces.

Platform

iOS · Digital

Repeat reason

Every game, rivalry, fantasy matchup or big NFL moment creates something new to make.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Lets fans make things with the artwork themselves, so every game-day post can feel a little more Corndoggy.

02

Corndoggy Stickers

Turn the existing sticker drawings into a collection fans can keep handy and use whenever they want.

Medium
How it works

1. The app starts with a small free Corndoggy pack.

2. Fans add the teams or themed packs they want.

3. Their stickers show up in Messages, Maker and widgets.

4. New drawings can arrive with updates or a simple refresh — no accounts needed.

Audience

NFL fans who already follow, save or buy Corndoggy art.

Players

Solo

Uses existing

NFL team stickers and existing drawings.

Needs new

Pack covers, app icon and occasional exclusive stickers.

Platform

iOS · Digital

Repeat reason

New packs, new art and more places to use the stickers.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Gives fans a little collection of drawings to keep, send and play with.

03

Corndoggy Bingo

Competitive NFL bingo for RedZone and live games.

Easy
How it works

1. Pick a limited number of football events before kickoff.

2. Common events score less; rare events score more.

3. Players mark events as they happen during RedZone or a live game.

4. Highest score wins. Example events: interception, missed PAT, 50+ yard TD, pick six, safety, overtime.

Audience

People watching NFL together, especially RedZone groups.

Players

2+

Uses existing

Reaction drawings and team characters.

Needs new

A small set of event illustrations or icons.

Platform

iOS · Digital

Repeat reason

A completely new board every NFL Sunday.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Turns watching football into a Corndoggy game without needing live scores, accounts or anything complicated.

04

Corndoggy Sunday Rules

A set of silly rules that react to whatever happens during the game.

Easy
How it works

1. Before kickoff, the group draws or generates a handful of Sunday Rules.

2. Each rule connects an NFL event to an action.

3. Interception → switch seats. Sack → 5 pushups. Missed kick → no snacks.

4. Keep the same rules all game or reshuffle between games.

Audience

Couples, friends, families and casual watch parties.

Players

2+

Uses existing

Reaction drawings and recognizable Corndoggy characters.

Needs new

Rule-card layouts and a few action/reaction drawings.

Platform

iOS first · Physical later

Repeat reason

Different rules and different people make every watch party play differently.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Pushes Corndoggy beyond looking at drawings and into the fun of watching football together.

05

Corndoggy Game Deck

Get rid of all your football event cards before everyone else.

Easy–Medium
How it works

1. Every player starts with a hand of random NFL event cards.

2. When your event happens — pick six, fumble, doink, safety or 50+ yard TD — discard it.

3. Trade one hopeless card for a new one every few minutes.

4. First out of cards wins. Pick the stakes before you start.

Audience

Watch parties and groups who want a simple game running in the background.

Players

2–8

Uses existing

Reaction art, characters and football drawings.

Needs new

Event-card artwork, stake cards and swap-token graphics.

Platform

iOS first · Physical version possible

Repeat reason

Random hands, different games and custom stakes make every session different.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Creates a real game around the artwork and could even become something you play with physical cards later.

06

Corndoggy Sunday Race

Everyone picks a team. Football events push their Corndoggy racer toward the finish.

Medium
How it works

1. Each person chooses a different NFL team and its Corndoggy character.

2. Tap your team’s events: touchdowns, sacks, turnovers and field goals.

3. Those events move the characters forward, backward, past each other or trigger little reactions.

4. One phone can run the whole race — no accounts or game server needed.

Audience

Groups watching RedZone who support different teams or want a casual alternative to fantasy.

Players

2–8

Uses existing

Team stickers become the racers.

Needs new

Race track, movement poses and a few win/lose reactions.

Platform

iOS · Digital

Repeat reason

Different teams and live games create a different race every Sunday.

Why it fits Corndoggy

Makes the team characters feel alive and gives a whole watch party one shared Corndoggy game.
