MIUNĀE

Eastern European beauty -- Made in Romania

↗Website ↗Brand Kit ↗Instagram

MIUNĀE needed to enter a saturated skincare category without resembling another polished wellness brand. Its formulas carried real history, but the story had to feel immediate rather than nostalgic. The work needed enough discipline to feel premium and enough tension to remain alive.

I built the identity around time as both ingredient and attitude. Typography, material choices and imagery were reduced until every element felt deliberate. The resulting system moves between quiet control and manipulated botanical overgrowth while staying recognizably MIUNĀE. Majority of brand, campaign and creative ideas were made in tandem with Linda.

AIMS

The most advanced AI Search for music catalogs

↗Website

AIMS is an AI search engine for music catalogs — sound, lyrics, video, searchable in seconds. It serves six different buyers, from production music companies to broadcasters, and the site had to speak to all of them without becoming a feature dump.

I built three distinct visual systems to separate what the company is, what it makes, and who it's for — company, products, and use cases each get their own logic. A color hierarchy that signals what matters where, and an interaction layer that ties it all together smoothly. From that foundation I built out the marketing and sales material — the same system carried straight through.
